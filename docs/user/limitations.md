# What it doesn't do yet

- **Word:** tracked changes can't be accepted or rejected. A comment covers text within one paragraph, and adding or removing comments isn't part of undo. When printing, table borders are left out.
- **PowerPoint:** videos are added as their thumbnail with a link, which is how a link to an online video travels between apps. In Workspace's slideshow they play on the slide; in PowerPoint or Keynote, clicking one opens the video in the browser. The slideshow plays slide transitions but not object animations.
- **PDF:** text, checkbox, radio and list form fields can be filled, and a signature field takes a picture of your signature; buttons and form scripts don't do anything, and Workspace can't add a certificate (digital) signature. Forms made only with Adobe's XFA show their placeholder page. Chinese, Japanese or Korean text in a font the PDF doesn't include (some older PDFs) may not show.
- **LaTeX:** needs TeX on the computer. A new, unsaved document can't use other files. Clicking in the PDF doesn't jump to the source (no SyncTeX yet). On Windows, installing and removing TeX are still being tested.
- **Excel** isn't supported yet.
