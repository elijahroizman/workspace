# What it doesn't do yet

- **Word:** tracked changes can't be accepted or rejected. A comment covers text within one paragraph, and adding or removing comments isn't part of undo. When printing, table borders are left out. Footnote bodies, block content controls such as some tables of contents, SmartArt, and Office Math equations may be missing from the page even though their original XML is kept on save. Right-to-left tables may display their columns in the wrong order.
- **PowerPoint:** videos are added as their thumbnail with a link, which is how a link to an online video travels between apps. In Writan's slideshow they play on the slide; in PowerPoint or Keynote, clicking one opens the video in the browser. The slideshow plays slide transitions but not object animations. Existing PowerPoint charts and SmartArt may appear as empty placeholders; their original parts are kept on save.
- **PDF:** text, checkbox, radio and list form fields can be filled, and a signature field takes a picture of your signature; buttons and form scripts don't do anything, and Writan can't add a certificate (digital) signature. Saving an unchanged PDF preserves its original bytes and digital signatures. Editing a signed PDF can invalidate its signature. Forms made only with Adobe's XFA show their placeholder page. Chinese, Japanese or Korean text in a font the PDF doesn't include (some older PDFs) may not show.
- **LaTeX:** needs TeX on the computer. A new, unsaved document can't use other files. Clicking in the PDF doesn't jump to the source (no SyncTeX yet). On Windows, installing and removing TeX are still being tested.
- **Word:** there's no spell check yet.
- **Excel:** pictures, conditional formatting and data validation aren't shown, but they're kept when the file is saved. Charts show and can be edited; radar, stock, bubble and surface charts show as a placeholder and are kept as they are unless you change them, and a chart you change is saved with Excel's default styling. A CSV file can't hold charts. About 140 of the common functions are worked out, including SUMIFS, XLOOKUP, IFS and the statistics and date functions; a formula using another one shows `#NAME?` until Excel recalculates it. A CSV file is saved with its values only, so formatting made in Writan is lost when it's closed.
- Inserting or deleting rows and columns updates formulas in the edited sheet and charts on every sheet. Formulas on other sheets that refer to those cells, and defined names, need manual updates.

- Session restore remembers saved file paths. It does not recover unsaved edits or untitled documents after a crash. Autosave and version history protect only changes that were successfully saved.

- An Office file with any individual XML part larger than 128 MiB or nested more than 256 elements deep is refused. This limit applies to internal XML parts, not the total file size or ordinary media attachments. Large documents can still use substantial memory.
